About

Thấy Rõ (Seeing Clearly) is a blog for ordinary people living ordinary lives — who want to understand why they suffer, why they react, and whether there’s a lighter way to be.

No “Buddhism”, “meditation” or “spirituality” labels here. Just observing reality — and sharing what’s seen, in ways you can apply to your morning meeting, family dinner, or a quiet moment alone at 11pm.

Every post has something you can try today. No background needed. Just a willingness to observe.
